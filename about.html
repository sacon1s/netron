ABOUT

What NETRON is, why it exists and how it works under the hood.

WHAT IS NETRON?

NETRON is a free, anonymous temporary email service. It gives you a disposable email address in seconds — no registration, no personal data, no strings attached. Use it wherever you'd rather not hand over your real mailbox: quick signups, one-time verifications, downloads, trials, Wi-Fi portals.

When you're done, the mailbox destroys itself. Your real inbox stays clean, your identity stays private.

HOW IT WORKS

Generate

Click “Generate”. NETRON creates a fresh mailbox for you — or use “Customize address” to pick your own name first.

Use it anywhere

Copy the address, or scan its QR code to move it to your phone. Paste it into any signup form.

Watch it live

The inbox refreshes every 10 seconds. New mail triggers a toast, a sound cue and an unread badge in the tab title.

Let it burn

After 60 minutes the mailbox and everything in it is destroyed automatically. Nothing to clean up.

PERFECT FOR

  • Signing up for services you don't fully trust yet
  • One-time confirmation emails and trial accounts
  • Downloading files gated behind an email form
  • Testing your own email flows during development
  • Keeping newsletters and promo spam away from your real address

TECHNOLOGY

NETRON is a fully static, client-side application — there is no NETRON server. Your browser talks directly to the public Guerrilla Mail API, which provides the disposable mailboxes. Everything else — favorites, statistics, your session — lives only in your browser's local storage.

The site is served from GitHub Pages and protected by a strict Content-Security-Policy. Email HTML is sanitized in the browser before it is ever rendered.

Please use NETRON for legitimate privacy needs. Don't use it for spam, fraud, harassment or anything illegal — see our Terms of Service.
