PRIVACY

Privacy Policy — last updated February 2026.

OUR COMMITMENT

NETRON is built with privacy as the core principle. The site has no backend, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. We believe in minimal data collection and maximum anonymity.

DATA WE COLLECT

We don't. There is no NETRON server receiving your data. The site is a static client; everything it shows you is fetched by your own browser directly from the mail provider. What stays in your browser (never transmitted anywhere by us):

  • Your current mailbox session (needed so the inbox survives page reloads)
  • Favorites you've starred
  • Your preferences (auto-refresh, sound) and simple usage counters

All of the above lives in your browser's local storage and can be wiped at any time by clearing site data.

DATA WE DON'T COLLECT

  • Personal identification information
  • IP addresses or location data
  • Cookies or trackers of any kind
  • Analytics, telemetry or ad data
  • Anything that is ever sold or shared — there is nobody to sell it to

THIRD PARTIES

Two services are involved in operating this site:

  • Guerrilla Mail API — hosts the disposable mailboxes and processes the email you receive there. Their own privacy policy applies to the mailbox content.
  • GitHub Pages — serves the static files of this site.

DATA RETENTION

Email is temporary by design: mailboxes and their contents are automatically destroyed 60 minutes after generation. NETRON keeps no records of your emails or activity.

EMAIL CONTENT WARNINGS

Disposable addresses are not private mailboxes. Anyone who knows the address may be able to read its mail while it exists. Never receive sensitive documents, passwords or personal data through NETRON.

CHANGES

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new “last updated” date.
